About Glow & Behold

Glow & Behold was founded out of necessity and a deep desire for change. As skincare professionals and enthusiasts, we were frustrated by the lack of truly simple, non-pore-clogging products on the market. We wanted something we could trust for ourselves and our clients—something natural, honest, and effective, without the heavy price tag.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make natural skincare accessible to everyone. We believe that you shouldn’t have to choose between quality and affordability. Every Glow & Behold product is crafted with carefully selected, plant-based ingredients that are gentle on your skin and kind to your wallet. We keep our formulas simple, transparent, and free from anything unnecessary.

Our Values

  • Simplicity: We use only what’s needed—no fillers, no harsh chemicals, no hidden surprises.
  • Affordability: Everyone deserves great skincare. We work hard to keep our products within reach, without compromising on quality.
  • Honesty: We’re transparent about every ingredient and every process. If it’s not good enough for us, it’s not good enough for you.
  • Kindness: To your skin, to our community, and to the planet. We choose sustainable practices and cruelty-free ingredients.

Our Team

We are a small, passionate team of skincare professionals, friends, and family. Our journey began with a single product made in a home kitchen, and has grown into a community of people who believe in the power of nature and the importance of self-care.

Sustainability

We are committed to minimising our environmental impact. Our packaging is recyclable, our ingredients are responsibly sourced, and we are always looking for new ways to do better for the earth.
