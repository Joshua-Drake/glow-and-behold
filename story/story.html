Our Story

Every great brand has a story to tell. Ours began not with a grand plan, but with a simple, stubborn need.

Borne of Necessity

As a skincare professional, I spent years searching for products that were truly gentle, non-pore-clogging, and uncomplicated. Time and again, I found myself frustrated by ingredient lists packed with fillers, heavy oils, and synthetic additives. I wanted something I could trust on my own skin—and even more importantly, on my clients’ skin. But every option seemed to compromise: either too harsh, too greasy, or simply too complex.

The Turning Point

The moment of change came after yet another client experienced a breakout from a so-called “natural” moisturiser. I realised I could no longer recommend products I didn’t fully believe in. If I couldn’t find a solution, I would have to create one myself. I set out to develop a formula that was simple, effective, and above all, kind to every skin type.

Creating Something Better

I started with a short list of ingredients I knew and trusted—botanical oils that wouldn’t clog pores, soothing extracts, and nothing unnecessary. After months of research, testing, and feedback from friends and clients, the first Glow & Behold product was born. It was light, breathable, and left skin feeling truly nourished—never weighed down.

Our Philosophy

Glow & Behold is built on the belief that skincare should be simple, honest, and safe for everyone. We never use pore-clogging ingredients, and every formula is designed with sensitivity and clarity in mind. If it’s not good enough for my own skin, it’s not good enough for my clients—or for you.

Today & Beyond

What began as a personal mission has grown into a community of people who value transparency and simplicity. We’re still guided by necessity: if we can’t find a product that meets our standards, we make it ourselves. Thank you for being part of our story.
