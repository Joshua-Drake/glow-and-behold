Blog: Learning to Formulate

Posted on 10 January 2026

Formulating skincare is both an art and a science. When I first started, I was overwhelmed by the sheer number of ingredients and the endless advice online. But I quickly learned that the best formulas start with a clear goal and a deep respect for simplicity.

My journey began with research—reading, watching, and listening to experts who valued gentle, effective, and honest skincare. I experimented with different oils, butters, and extracts, always keeping in mind the needs of sensitive skin. There were plenty of failures, but each one taught me something new about how ingredients interact and what skin truly needs.

One of the most important lessons was that less is often more. By focusing on a few high-quality, non-comedogenic ingredients, I could create products that were both safe and effective. The process of learning to formulate has been humbling, but it’s also been incredibly rewarding to see the difference it makes for real people.
