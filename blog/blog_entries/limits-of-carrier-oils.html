Blog: The Limits of Carrier Oils

Posted on 24 January 2026

Carrier oils are the backbone of many natural skincare products. They provide nourishment, help deliver active ingredients, and can be wonderfully soothing. But as I learned more about formulation, I discovered that carrier oils also have their limits.

Not all oils are created equal. Some, like jojoba and squalane, closely mimic the skin’s natural sebum and are less likely to clog pores. Others, such as coconut oil, can be too heavy for certain skin types and may contribute to breakouts. It’s important to understand the comedogenic rating of each oil and to test how your skin responds.

Carrier oils alone can’t address every skin concern. They lack the targeted actives—like antioxidants, vitamins, and peptides—that can help with issues such as aging, pigmentation, or sensitivity. That’s why a balanced formula often combines the best of both worlds: gentle carrier oils and carefully chosen actives.

Formulating with respect for the limits of carrier oils has helped me create products that are both safe and effective. It’s a reminder that even the most natural ingredients need to be used thoughtfully and in harmony with the skin’s needs.
